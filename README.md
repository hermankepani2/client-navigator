# Client Navigator

A human-led workspace by **Kepani Digital** for reviewing a business, identifying evidence-backed ways Kepani Digital may help, and preparing a respectful first conversation.

> **AI proposes. Herman decides the next step.**

## Project purpose

Client Navigator helps organize a small-business review and assess possible fit for:

- Paid social advertising
- AI automation and governance consulting
- Digital-presence support

It uses information supplied by the user or verifiable public sources. It makes evidence gaps and uncertainty visible and treats recommendations and outreach as drafts for human review.

## Workflow

1. **Intake** — capture the business and the question being reviewed.
2. **Evidence** — gather user-provided or verifiable public-source information.
3. **Review** — distinguish observed facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
4. **Fit assessment** — consider relevant Kepani Digital services without overstating need or fit.
5. **Outreach draft** — prepare a respectful first-contact message.
6. **Human decision** — Herman verifies the facts and decides whether to use or send the draft.

## Operating principles

- Do not invent business facts, needs, contact history, or performance data.
- Keep sources and uncertainty visible.
- Treat AI output as a recommendation or draft, not a decision.
- Require human review before outreach is used or sent.
- Keep communications accurate, respectful, and appropriate to the business.
